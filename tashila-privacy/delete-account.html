Request Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data from Tashila Client (com.tashila.tashilaclient) and Tashila Driver (com.tashila.tashiladriver).

How Account Deletion Works

You may request account deletion at any time, either directly within the mobile application or through the automated web request form below without needing to reinstall the app.

🗑️

What Is Deleted

Your phone number, profile avatar, driver license documents, vehicle registration card, GPS location logs, ratings, and push notification tokens.

📂

What May Be Retained

Past fiscal invoices, trip payment receipts, and anonymized aggregate telemetry strictly necessary to satisfy statutory tax and commercial legal obligations.

⏱️

Processing Timeline

Account access is deactivated immediately upon request. All associated personal records are permanently erased within 30 calendar days.

Important Note for Tashila Drivers

If you are a registered driver, deleting your account will permanently cancel your verified driver status, your vehicle profile, and your access to transport requests. Any pending payout or balance must be settled prior to account termination.

Deleting Directly Inside the Mobile App

If you have the mobile application installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the Tashila or Tashila Driver app.
  2. Navigate to your Profile tab from the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap on "Settings" (الإعدادات) > "Security & Account".
  4. Select "Delete Account" (حذف الحساب) and confirm with your OTP code.
