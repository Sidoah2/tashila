Overview & Scope

Welcome to Tashila ("we", "us", or "our"). Tashila operates an on-demand freight and logistics transportation technology platform that connects customers needing cargo transportation ("Clients") with independent transport providers operating single-cabin and double-cabin utility trucks ("Drivers").

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tashila Client mobile application (com.tashila.tashilaclient), the Tashila Driver mobile application (com.tashila.tashiladriver), our website, APIs, and associated online services (collectively, the "Services").

By downloading, accessing, or using any of our applications, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

02

Information We Collect

To provide safe, reliable, and efficient logistics services, we collect information directly provided by you, automatically generated during app usage, and gathered from third-party partners.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Registration Details: When you register as a Client or Driver, we collect your mobile phone number for One-Time Password (OTP) verification, full name, profile picture, and language preference.
  • Driver Verification Documents (KYC): For Tashila Driver accounts, we collect and verify statutory identification documents including government-issued driver's license, vehicle registration papers ("Carte Grise"), vehicle insurance certificates, vehicle type classification (Single Cabin or Double Cabin pickup truck), and vehicle license plate number.
  • Communications & Support: Records of inquiries, dispute reports, customer service chats, and ratings or feedback provided after completing trips.

B. Information Automatically Collected

  • Precise & Coarse Location Data: Real-time GPS coordinates collected from your mobile device (see Section 3 for detailed disclosure).
  • Device & Hardware Identifiers: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifier (Android ID), network carrier, IP address, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens used to send order alerts.
  • Transaction & Order History: Pickup coordinates, delivery destination, timestamp of request, completion time, truck category chosen, calculated distance, and agreed fare.
03

Location Data & Background Use Disclosure

Prominent Disclosure: Location Access in Tashila & Tashila Driver

Location access is essential to the core functionality of Tashila freight transportation. Below we explicitly detail how location is gathered for both apps, including background location tracking.

1. Tashila Client App (Foreground Location)

The Client app uses ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION exclusively while the application is active on screen (foreground) to:

  • Automatically set your current cargo pickup pin on the interactive map.
  • Display nearby active drivers and available truck types (Single Cabin / Double Cabin).
  • Estimate travel route distances and trip pricing through Google Maps API.

2. Tashila Driver App (Foreground & Background Location)

The Tashila Driver app collects precise location data via the BackgroundService even when the application is running in the background or when the screen is closed, under the following strict conditions:

  • Dispatch & Order Matching: While a driver has turned their status to "Online", continuous background location enables our server algorithm to match nearby customer shipment requests with the most optimal driver, preventing wasted fuel and lengthy wait times.
  • Real-Time Transit & Safety Tracking: During an accepted and ongoing delivery mission, background location tracking transmits accurate transit telemetry to the customer and dispatch console. This ensures cargo safety, transparency, accurate arrival estimates, and verification of pickup and drop-off milestones.
Driver Control Over Location

Drivers maintain full control over background location tracking. When a driver toggles the status switch to "Offline", background location reporting immediately ceases completely.

Our applications request access to the device camera (android.permission.CAMERA) and media storage under explicit user confirmation for the following exclusive purposes:

📷

Driver KYC Verification

Drivers photograph their valid driver's license and vehicle registration document during onboarding so administrators can review and approve roadworthiness.

👤

Profile Avatar Upload

Users may upload a profile picture from their gallery or camera to identify themselves to drivers and clients for seamless order handoff.

🔒

Secure Cloud Storage

Uploaded photos are transmitted over encrypted TLS connections to secured cloud buckets (Cloudinary) and are never used for marketing purposes.

05

How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected data solely for legitimate business operations, specifically:

  • Matching freight requests with suitable drivers in the vicinity based on truck size (single vs. double cabin).
  • Facilitating direct in-app or phone dialer communication between assigned Driver and Client to coordinate loading and unloading.
  • Generating transparent fare estimates, trip receipts, and trip route logs.
  • Investigating customer disputes, vehicle breakdowns, or safety concerns.
  • Sending critical push alerts regarding ride acceptance, driver arrival, or security notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Complying with applicable legal, fiscal, and transportation regulatory requirements.

07

Data Retention & Security Standards

Security: All communication between Tashila mobile apps and our backend servers occurs over modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3). Sensitive credentials and access tokens are hashed and securely stored.

Retention: We retain user personal data for as long as the account remains active. If you request account deletion, we immediately deactivate the account and delete personal identifiers, location logs, and profile assets within 30 business days, retaining only anonymized telemetry or statutory fiscal invoice records required by local commercial law.

08

Account & Data Deletion Policy

In strict adherence to Google Play Store requirements, users have the unconditional right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Method 1 (In-App): Navigate to Profile > Settings > Delete Account inside either the Tashila Client or Tashila Driver app and confirm the verification prompt.
  2. Method 2 (Web Form): Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Web Page to submit an automated deletion request with your registered phone number.
  3. Method 3 (Direct Email): Send an email from your registered email address or citing your phone number to support@tashila.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
What Happens Upon Deletion

Account deletion is permanent. Your phone profile, avatar, driver licensing documents, vehicle records, rating history, and location logs will be purged permanently from our active production databases.

09

Children's Privacy Protection

Our Services are strictly directed toward adults and business operators capable of entering legally binding commercial transport contracts. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 18 (and specifically under 13). If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will immediately delete that information from our records.

Your Legal Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we maintain about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete profile or vehicle information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent removal of your data as outlined in Section 8.
  • Revocation of Permissions: Revoke Location or Camera permissions at any time via your Android device OS settings (Settings > Apps > Tashila > Permissions).
