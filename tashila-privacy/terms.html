Platform Relationship & Scope

Tashila is an on-demand technology platform connecting customers requiring cargo transportation ("Clients") with independent commercial transport operators ("Drivers"). Tashila provides digital dispatch, route estimation, and communication tools. Tashila itself is not a common carrier or transport company; transportation agreements are concluded directly between Client and Driver.

Driver Obligations & Compliance

Registered Drivers agree to:

  • Possess a valid, unexpired commercial driver's license suitable for their registered utility truck category (Single Cabin / Double Cabin).
  • Maintain valid road insurance, vehicle inspection certificates, and technical safety standards.
  • Comply with all road traffic safety laws and speed limits.
  • Operate with background location active during online hours to receive and navigate shipments accurately.
