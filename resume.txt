Step-by-Step E2E Testing Instructions
1. Open the Admin Panel
Go to: https://tashilaadmin-tau.vercel.app
Log in with:
Email: admin@tashila.com
Password: TashilaAdmin2026!
(Verify that you can see the maps loading and the pricing models in the settings).
2. Test the Driver & Client Mobile Apps
Launch the Apps: Run both the Client and Driver apps on your emulator or physical device.
Register a Client:
Open the Client App and sign up with a phone number.
Since we enabled testing mode, use the OTP code 111111 to log in.
Register a Driver:
Open the Driver App and sign up.
Upload test documents (images) for the license and vehicle registration (these will upload to your Cloudinary storage successfully).
Verify using the OTP code 111111.
Approve the Driver:
Once the driver is registered, they will be in pending status.
Log into the Admin Panel (https://tashilaadmin-tau.vercel.app), navigate to the Drivers tab, select the pending driver, and click Approve.
Go Online & Request a Ride:
In the Driver App, turn the online switch ON.
In the Client App, select a destination on the map and request a single-cabin or double-cabin truck.
The Driver App should receive the real-time ride offer popup immediately!
